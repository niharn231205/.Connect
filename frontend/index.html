A few things to know.

Facts about how the studio operates.

What resume formats are supported?

We accept PDF, DOCX, and TXT files up to 5 MB in size.

How do the resume checks work?

Google Gemini checks whether your file is a personal resume or CV. Experiment reports and assignments are rejected. A separate structure check suggests improvements.

Is the analysis score an ATS guarantee?

No. The score checks common resume sections and keywords; it does not predict hiring decisions. Gemini checks document type, not whether the claims in a resume are true.

Can beginners use this?

Yes. The platform is designed for anyone looking to organize their professional presentation, regardless of current experience level.
